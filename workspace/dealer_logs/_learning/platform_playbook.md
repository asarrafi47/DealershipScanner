## carscommerce (Dealer Inspire) group accounts — identify the store by its feed id (2026-09-24 15:07Z)
- One `ccid` serves the whole group (Hendrick: 11,000+ cars, 15+ rooftops). `dealer.location` per listing is free text: `City, ST`, or `street<br/>City, ST ZIP<br/>phone`; `extra_fields.custom_location` names the selling store on some accounts.
- The store's own identity is on its page: `"dealername"`, `"oem_code"` (GM BAC etc.), and each listing's `source_id`. The OEM-code feed is often the small NEW-only feed; the DMS feed (numeric, 9048741-style) or a named feed (`MallofGeorgiaMazda`) holds the rest. Merge feed ids that stamp the same street or store name; never merge on a bare `City, ST`.
- `custom_text_N` meanings differ per account; the page's field map (`"Location":"custom_text_25"`) says which is the Location facet. A Location value covers every same-town rooftop (Cary, NC = 3 stores) — verify any filter by replaying it and demanding ONE rooftop.
- Evidence: hendrickbuickgmccary-com, rickhendrickchevynaples-com, mallofgamazda-com, stevensonhendrickhonda-com, stevensonhendrickmazda-com, chevyusa-com.

## Store filter, round four (2026-09-26 08:20Z)
- Tutton CDJR landed 346/346 only after the scope marker rode INSIDE the payload (`_feed_scoped`): dealer_run re-parses replayed bodies twice more without the recipe. The CarsCommerce API 400s on any unknown body key, so scope is recognised from the body's own shape: `facetFilters.source_id`, or a single custom_text value equal to the roster/site name.
- Single-store accounts: when every site-mapped Location facet holds ONE value, the whole account is the store — no filter (Napleton Honda of Morton Grove: 49 → 393). Group accounts: the facet the site names "Location" outranks size (Group 1 Toyota North Austin: meta_location tagged 1,000 group cars with the store's name; Location = the store's own set) and becomes a second recipe next to the feed-id one.

## Store filter, round five (2026-09-26 09:10Z)
- Single-store accounts can have EMPTY Location facets (Napleton Honda of Morton Grove). Rule: identity feed + every other feed carries no contradicting stamp (no other place, no other store name, no other street) + account ≤ 1,000 cars → the whole account is the store, no filter. Lot notes ("TOW/JORGE R/367676": slash or digit-heavy) never count as store names. Verified against Hendrick controls (Cary 442, Stevenson Honda 455 unchanged).
