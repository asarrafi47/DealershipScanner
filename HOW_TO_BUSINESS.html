The Problem We Solve

Finding the right car is frustrating:

  • ❌ Information scattered across 100+ dealership websites
  • ❌ No standardized specs or reliability data
  • ❌ Hard to compare similar vehicles
  • ❌ Can't see market trends (is this a good price?)
  • ❌ Time-consuming manual research

Our Solution

We collect, organize, and intelligently search vehicle data:

  • ✅ All vehicles in one place (dealerships + private sellers)
  • ✅ Complete specs automatically pulled and verified
  • ✅ AI-powered comparisons and analysis
  • ✅ Market intelligence (pricing trends, fair value, demand)
  • ✅ Fast, smart search that understands intent

Real-World Example

How a 2024 BMW ends up searchable:

  1. A dealership in Irvine posts a new 2024 BMW i7 on their website
  2. Our crawler discovers it within 24 hours
  3. We extract: price ($95,000), mileage (2,000 mi), color (silver), etc.
  4. Our enrichment adds: EPA fuel economy (23 MPG combined), features, reliability data
  5. We generate an embedding (AI-friendly "fingerprint") so searches like "luxury electric sedan" find it
  6. User searches "luxury car under $100k" → finds this BMW ranked #3
  7. User clicks "Chat about this car" → AI tells them about efficiency, features, dealer reviews
  8. User buys plan upgrade to unlock full market intelligence → we make revenue
💡 Key Point: Everything happens automatically. Users don't have to visit 10 different websites — the data comes to them, organized and intelligent.

Frequently Asked Questions

For Car Shoppers

How current is the inventory?

Our crawler runs 24/7. Most vehicles appear within 24 hours of being posted on a dealer's website. Prices and availability update daily. For the most current info, we always link to the dealer's website.

Is Sarrafi Collection actually free?

Yes! Search, view details, and save up to 5 cars for free forever. Premium subscription ($9.99/month) unlocks AI chat, market intelligence, and unlimited saved cars.

Can I contact dealers directly?

Absolutely. Every listing includes the dealer's website, phone, and contact form. We provide links to help you reach out directly.

How does the AI chat know about cars?

Our AI (Claude) is trained on vast automotive knowledge. Combined with real-time data from our database (specs, reviews, market prices), it gives expert-level insights.

Is my search data private?

Yes. We don't sell search data to dealers or third parties. Your privacy is paramount. See our full privacy policy for details.

For Dealers

How does inventory sync work?

Our crawler visits your website daily and automatically extracts new vehicles. No setup required on your end. Just keep your site updated, and we'll reflect changes within 24 hours.

Do I have to pay?

No! Basic listing is free forever. Premium dealer plan ($99-499/month) adds analytics, priority placement, and lead management tools.

How do I know if customers are interested in my cars?

With Premium, you get a dashboard showing views, clicks, and lead info. See which vehicles get the most interest and optimize your inventory accordingly.

What if I stop using Sarrafi Collection?

No contract needed. Cancel anytime. Your inventory will stop appearing within 24 hours. Reactivate whenever you want.

Do you handle customer communications?

Not directly. We bring qualified buyers to your dealership. You manage communication and sales. We provide lead tracking tools to help you follow up.

For Investors / Partners

What's the addressable market?

US used car market: ~35M transactions/year, $400B+ total value. Sarrafi Collection targets the digital discovery and shopping phase, where AI can add significant value.

What are unit economics?

Consumer: CAC ~$2-5, LTV $100-300 (assuming 20-month lifetime). Dealer: CAC ~$50-100, LTV $1000-5000+. Payback within 6-12 months.

Who are the competitors?

Direct: Cars.com, Edmunds, Autotrader (aggregators), Carvana (direct sales). Indirect: Google Search, dealer websites, Facebook Marketplace. Our AI-first approach is novel.

What's your go-to-market strategy?

Phase 1 (now): Organic growth + SEO, freemium conversion. Phase 2: Dealer partnerships and affiliate networks. Phase 3: OEM partnerships and data licensing.

What are the biggest risks?

Market: Dealers switching to direct sales channels. Tech: API changes at dealer sites, web scraping detection. Competition: Larger players entering AI-powered auto search. We mitigate through patents, scale, and partnership depth.
