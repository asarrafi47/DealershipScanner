{# The stylesheet partials that style.css used to pull in with @import. @import serialises stylesheet discovery: the browser cannot even learn a partial exists until style.css has been fetched AND parsed, and every one of these is render-blocking. Measured on the car page with a warm HTTP cache at 12 Mbit/s / 40ms RTT: style.css occupied 469-511ms, then all 14 partials ran 511-697ms (PerformanceResourceTiming initiatorType "css"), and first contentful paint landed at 712ms. The document itself had arrived in 1ms. Emitting them as sibling tags lets the preload scanner start all of them with the document instead of a round trip later. ORDER IS THE CASCADE. This matches style.css's @import order exactly, which was itself the order the rules appeared in before the split. Do NOT reorder these. 14-vdp-uniform.css stays last on purpose -- it normalises the car detail page's card surfaces and heading scale over the per-section rules above it. #}
