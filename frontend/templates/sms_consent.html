The Sarrafi Cars sign-in program

Sarrafi Cars sends one-time login codes by text message to the mobile number you type on the sign-in page. It sends nothing else: no marketing and no alerts.

What you agree to

On the sign-in page you tick an unticked box next to this text before we send a code:

How often and what it costs

One message each time you ask for a code, and never otherwise. Message and data rates may apply, depending on your mobile plan.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP to any message to opt out; you may get one message confirming it, then no more. Reply START to opt back in. While you are opted out we cannot text you a code.

Your number
