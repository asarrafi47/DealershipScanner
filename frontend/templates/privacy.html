Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ legal_last_updated }}. Version {{ legal_version }}.

This policy explains what Sarrafi Cars keeps about you, why, who helps us run the site, how long things are kept and how to have them deleted.

What we keep

  • Your phone number. We treat it as personal data. It is stored in full because we need it to text you a sign-in code and to show you its last digits. We also store when you agreed to receive codes, which version of the consent text you agreed to, and the line type (mobile, landline or internet number) the number had when it was checked.
  • Your account. A username, an email address if you gave one, your role, and for staff a password stored as a one-way hash. The account database is encrypted.
  • What you save and do on the site. Saved cars and searches, hidden dealerships, recent searches, cars you viewed or compared, and comments and reviews you post.
  • Counters for limits. To stop abuse we count sign-in codes and chat messages per phone number, device and network address. These counters store a one-way hash of the number or address, never the number or address itself, and never the text of a message.
  • Cookies. A signed sign-in cookie, a security token for forms, and a random device cookie used only to count requests for rate limits.

We never see or keep your sign-in codes (our text-message provider creates and checks them) and we do not keep the text of the messages sent to you.

How we use your phone number

Only to send you sign-in codes and to tell your account apart from others. We do not use it for marketing and we do not text you anything you did not ask for.

No sale and no sharing for marketing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not sell or share your phone number or opt-in data with third parties for marketing.

Who helps us run the site

These companies process data for us, each only for the purpose listed:

  • Twilio: sends and checks sign-in codes by text message, and looks up a number's line type.
  • Cloudflare: the Turnstile check against automated sign-ups (it reads signals from your browser) and the network that delivers the site.
  • Railway: hosts the site, its databases and its logs.
  • Our email provider: sends account emails such as email confirmation and password reset.
  • Our chat model provider: receives the messages you type to the car assistant, with the listing details needed to answer them.

Each provider keeps its own records under its own policy. [The owner confirms each provider's retention before approval.]

What a seller's listing shows

A private seller's listing shows the car, its photos and description, and a ZIP code area. It never shows the seller's street address, phone number or email address.

How long we keep things

  • Sign-in code requests: 7 days after they end.
  • Records of each code we sent: 30 days.
  • Records of wrong code attempts: 24 hours after the lock they caused ends.
  • Chat usage counters: 35 days.
  • Your phone number and sign-in identity: until your account is deleted.
  • Server logs: as long as our hosting provider keeps them. [The owner confirms this period.] Logs never contain your phone number or a code.

A nightly job deletes records once they pass these ages.

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account through the contact below. Account data is deleted within 30 days of a request. Rate-limit counters keyed by a one-way hash are kept up to 35 days, because deleting them early would reset the limits. Backups are overwritten on the provider's schedule. [The owner states the backup retention.]

Children

You must be 13 or older to use Sarrafi Cars. We do not knowingly keep data about anyone younger; if you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Security

The account database is encrypted, limits are keyed by one-way hashes and sign-in codes are never stored by us. No system is perfectly secure.

Changes to this policy

The date and version at the top change when we update this policy. If the text-message consent wording changes, we ask for your agreement again the next time you sign in.
