Privacy Policy

Last updated {{ legal_last_updated }}. Version {{ legal_version }}.

This policy explains what Sarrafi Cars keeps about you, why, who receives it, how long things are kept and how to have them deleted. Sarrafi Cars is run by [OPERATOR NAME], [OPERATOR POSTAL ADDRESS].

What we keep

  • Your account. A username, an email address, your role, and a password stored as a one-way hash. If you sign in with Google or Apple, the account id that service gives us. If you sign in with a phone number, the account gets a made-up placeholder email address. For accounts that use an authenticator app for two-step sign-in, the app's secret. If paid plans are on and you pay, the Stripe customer and subscription ids for your account.
  • Your phone number, if you sign in with one. We treat it as personal data. It is stored in full because we need it to text you a sign-in code and to show you its last digits. We also store when you agreed to receive codes, which version of the consent text you agreed to, and the line type (mobile, landline or internet number) the number had when it was checked.
  • What you save and do on the site. Saved cars and searches, hidden dealerships, your recent searches, cars you viewed or compared, and comments, photos and reviews you post.
  • Searches. Each search on the site (its text, filters, ZIP code and state, and the number of results) is recorded with your account id if you are signed in, or with a random session key if you are not.
  • Counters for limits. To stop abuse we count sign-in codes and chat messages per account, phone number, device and network address. These counters store a one-way hash of the number or address, never the number or address itself, and never the text of a message. Comments, reviews and the reports you file store a one-way hash of the network address they came from.
  • Cookies and your browser. A signed sign-in cookie, a security token for forms, and a random device cookie used to count requests for limits. Your browser keeps, in its own storage on your device: the sidebar assistant's recent conversation and last search, the ZIP code found from your location, your compare list, finance calculator inputs and display settings.

We never see or keep your sign-in codes (our text-message provider creates and checks them) and we do not keep the text of the messages sent to you.

What others can see

A review you post shows your username unless you tick "Post anonymously". If you signed up with Google or Apple, your username starts as the part of your email address before the "@". A private seller's listing shows the car, its photos and description, and a ZIP code area. It never shows the seller's street address, phone number or email address.

How we use your phone number

Only to send you sign-in codes and to tell your account apart from others. We do not use it for marketing and we do not text you anything you did not ask for.

No sale and no sharing for marketing

We do not sell your personal data. We do not sell or share your phone number or opt-in data with third parties for marketing.

How long we keep things

  • Sign-in code requests: 7 days after they end.
  • Records of each code we sent: 30 days.
  • Records of wrong code attempts: 24 hours after the lock they caused ends.
  • A number's line type check: 30 days.
  • Chat usage counters: 35 days.
  • Recent searches on your account: the last 100; you can clear them at any time.
  • Search records, and the cars you viewed or compared: [RETENTION PERIOD FOR SEARCH RECORDS AND VIEW AND COMPARE HISTORY].
  • Your account, phone number and sign-in identity: until your account is deleted.
  • Server logs: as long as our hosting provider keeps them, [SERVER LOG RETENTION PERIOD]. Logs never contain your phone number or a code. If an account email cannot be sent, the log can contain the email address. When the assistant refuses or cuts short an answer, the first 120 characters of your message can be written to the log.

The site deletes chat counters once they pass that age. Sign-in records are deleted by a cleanup job once they pass these ages. [OWNER: schedule the phone sign-in cleanup job before phone sign-in is turned on.]

Deleting your data

You can ask us to delete your account through the contact below. Account data is deleted within 30 days of a request. Rate-limit counters keyed by a one-way hash are kept up to 35 days, because deleting them early would reset the limits. Backups are overwritten on the provider's schedule, [BACKUP RETENTION PERIOD]. A review keeps the name it was posted under until the review itself is deleted.

Children

You must be 13 or older to use Sarrafi Cars. We do not knowingly keep data about anyone younger; if you believe a child under 13 has an account, tell us and we will delete it.

Security

In production, the database that holds accounts (usernames, email addresses and password hashes), search records and view and compare history is encrypted on disk with a key the application holds. Everything else, including phone numbers, saved cars and searches, hidden dealerships, recent searches, reviews, comments and photos, private sellers' listings and the limit counters, is kept in a separate database that the application does not encrypt itself.
